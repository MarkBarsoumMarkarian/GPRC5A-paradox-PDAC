# Legacy outputs

Files directly under `results/tables/` and `results/figures/` were generated by the original five-aim analysis and are retained only for auditability. They are superseded and must not be used as evidence for a subtype reversal, Simpson's paradox, causal treatment effect, or independent machine-learning prediction.

Current results are under `results/v2/` and are rebuilt by `R/v2/run_v2.R`.
